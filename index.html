01 / About

Research-driven, field-grounded.

Md. Habib Hasan Himel

Graduate · Plant Breeding & Sustainable Agriculture

I am an agriculture graduate currently pursuing an MS in Genetics and Plant Breeding at Sher-e-Bangla Agricultural University (SAU), Dhaka. My work sits at the intersection of field experimentation, phenotypic evaluation, and data-driven crop improvement.

I have hands-on experience designing and managing field trials, greenhouse studies, and laboratory analysis — with a particular focus on genetic variability and characterization of blackgram (Vigna mungo), including multi-environment trials and abiotic stress evaluation. I use R and Python for statistical modeling and data management, and I care deeply about climate-smart, sustainable agricultural practices that strengthen food security.

Beyond research, I communicate science clearly, coordinate projects, and contribute to outreach on agricultural innovation and the SDGs. Open to scholarships, research assistantships, and roles that match these abilities.

Focus areas

Genetic variability in legumes, multi-environment trials, plant physiology & stress, sustainable agriculture, and data-driven crop improvement.

Currently focused on characterization of blackgram (Vigna mungo) and evaluation under abiotic stress conditions.
